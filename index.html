      Paper Day-Trading Lab (skill-building only, no real money)

      Nobody reliably predicts markets. The goal here is to practice following rules and to measure results honestly. No profit is expected or promised.

      Strategy: 15-min Opening Range Breakout (ORB) + VWAP filter

      Universe: SPY, QQQ, and large caps (avg volume > 5M shares/day, price > $20). 1. Opening range (OR): the high and low from 9:30 to 9:45 ET. No trades before 9:45. 2. Filters (all must pass): - OR width is between 0.2% and 1.0% of price (skip ranges that are too tight or too wide). - Longs only when price is above VWAP, shorts only when price is below VWAP. - No new entries within 15 min of a scheduled macro event or Fed speaker. - On CPI/FOMC days, use the 30-min OR (9:30-10:00) instead. - Skip a stock that has earnings that day (ETFs are fine). 3. Entry: a 5-min candle CLOSES beyond the OR high (long) or OR low (short). Enter at that close. One entry per ticker per direction. 4. Stop: the opposite side of the OR, or the OR midpoint if that is tighter than 0.5%. Never move the stop farther away. 5. Target: 2R (2 × entry-to-stop distance). Optional: once at 1R, move the stop to breakeven. 6. Time stop: no new entries after 11:30 ET. Exit everything by 15:50 ET. 7. Sizing: - Risk 1% of balance per trade: shares = floor((0.01 × balance) / |entry − stop|). - Max position cap: position value ≤ 100% of balance (no margin) AND ≤ 1,000 shares. Use whichever gives FEWER shares. 8. Daily limits: max 3 trades a day, and stop for the day after 2 losses or after −2% on the day.

      Files

      Download the raw data and tools behind this site.

      • journal.csv: one row per trade. Mark planned ideas with exit_reason=PLANNED until they're filled or cancelled.
      • state.json: starting and current balance. Update it after each closed trade.
      • stats.py: python3 stats.py prints stats and writes equity_curve.png.

      Review process

      • Daily (5 min, after the close): fill every journal field. Grade each trade A to F on process, not P&L. Write 1 lesson. Note if you broke a rule and why.
      • Weekly (Fri): run stats.py. Write the top 3 lessons, your most common rule break, and one thing to practice next week. Write these under "Weekly Reviews" below.
      • Judge the strategy only after 50+ closed trades. Benchmarks:
      • Expectancy above 0R (positive), and profit factor above 1.2.
      • Rule adherence at least 90%. If it's lower, the results measure you, not the strategy. Fix discipline first.
      • Max drawdown ≤ 10% of starting balance. If you hit −10%, pause and review before trading again.
      • Tweaking: change ONE variable at a time (e.g., OR length 15→30, target 2R→1.5R, VWAP filter on/off). Run each version 50+ trades, and log the version in the setup column (e.g., ORB15-v1). Never tweak in the middle of a sample just because of a losing streak.

      Copy Trading Research (2026-10-14)

      Research only. No trading, wallets, or money.

      1. copyfomo and FOMO

      • FOMO (fomo.family) is a social trading app with public trader leaderboards. Its spot fee is reportedly about 0.50% with a minimum of about $0.95. FOMO doesn't publish that rate, so treat it as reported (https://www.copyfomo.com/blog/fomo-app-fees).
      • copyfomo is a separate, independent Telegram bot (@copyfomo_bot). It copies FOMO leaderboard traders' buys AND sells into your own non-custodial wallet. Chains: Robinhood Chain, Base, BNB, Solana. You can size per trade as a flat amount or a % of the leader's trade, with a cap (https://www.copyfomo.com/docs, https://www.copyfomo.com/).
      • Fee: 2% per copied buy and per copied sell (1.8% with a referral), plus gas. That's about double the usual 1% for bots (https://degenbench.com/en/tools/copyfomo, DefiLlama adapter https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DefiLlama/dimension-adapters/refs/heads/master/fees/copyfomo.ts). Round trip: about 4% in copyfomo fees, plus FOMO's own fee.
      • Lag: copyfomo says it fills "about a second" behind the leader. Its own measurement found a median +39% price move one minute after a leader's first buy, so even short delays cost far more than fees (copyfomo.com and its fomo-app-fees blog post).
      • Their referral program pays 15% of your referrals' fees. Much of the hype around it is affiliate-driven (docs).
      • Launched Sept 2026. About $0.20M in fees over 30 days (DegenBench, citing DefiLlama).

      2. Other tools and how people find wallets

      • GMGN (https://gmgn.ai/): multi-chain (Solana, BSC, Base, ETH). It has auto copy-trading with sizing, slippage, and TP/SL settings, a "Rank" wallet leaderboard, a "Radar" that finds wallets behind past winning tokens, and wallet PnL and win-rate analytics (https://gmgn.ai/blog/how-to-track-copy-solana-smart-money/). These sources are GMGN's own blog, so they're biased.
      • Axiom: a Solana terminal with wallet tracking and alerts. It has NO verified auto-copy, so you trade by hand (https://madeonsol.com/blog/best-solana-copy-trading-bots).
      • Photon: a manual Solana terminal with no copy trading (https://gmgn.ai/blog/best-meme-coin-trading-platforms-2026/).
      • Trojan: auto copy trading (same GMGN comparison). Cielo: wallet intel across 30+ chains, from Free up to $199/mo (madeonsol).
      • Kolscan: free Solana tracker of influencer (KOL) trades. It only tracks and can't execute trades (search synthesis, not opened directly, so UNVERIFIED detail).
      • FOMO leaderboard mirrors: https://provadata.com/leaderboard/7d (checks wallets on-chain), https://fomolens.app/leaderboard, https://www.fomoapi.dev/leaderboard
      • Finding wallets: use leaderboards (the weakest method), or reverse-search early buyers of past real winners and favor wallets that hit several of them (GMGN Radar method).

      5. Risk rules

      • Why copying lags: you always buy after the leader, and often you buy into their exit. MEV bots sandwich predictable copy orders, so every entry and exit fills worse (https://memecentral.fun/guides/copy-trading-memecoin-wallets, https://nexflowscan.com/guides/copy-trading-rug-mechanics/).
      • Leaderboards favor luck. Check trade count and time period, and see if the record still holds without the single best trade (memecentral).
      • Farmed wallets: an operator launches its own token, the "trader" wallet buys early, the operator pumps it, and then dumps on the copiers. Screenshots and videos prove nothing. No verifiable address means it's marketing (nexflowscan).
      • Bot or fake-PnL signs: win rate above 70-75% over 50+ trades, repeated round trips under 10 minutes, lots of failed transactions, airdrops counted as profit, wallets sharing a funder (https://madeonsol.com/blog/how-to-filter-bot-wallets-kol-list-solana, https://apm.fun/blog/how-to-identify-real-smart-money-wallets-on-chain).
      • Rug sign: 3+ wallets buying in the same block and holding a large share of supply (24 wallets holding 76% will almost surely go to 0) (https://docs.trench.bot/bundle-tools/bundle-scanner-guide).
      • Sizing: never match the leader's size. Use a small flat ticket with a cap. Tiny tickets get eaten by minimum fees (FOMO's ~$0.95 minimum means ~1.9% per leg on small trades, per copyfomo's blog). Only use money you can lose entirely. Keep the 2-loss fire rule. Never share seed phrases.

      Small Account Strategy Research (2026-10-14)

      Research only. No trading, wallets, or signups. Goal: grow $10-$20. Note on $ math: one SOL-priced source implies SOL ≈ $170 (0.001 SOL = $0.17, https://trader.scorplabs.online/blog/pump-fun-sniper-bot-how-to-snipe-solana-launches). I used that to turn SOL fees into dollars, so treat those $ figures as estimates.

      Cost per trade at $10-$20, odds, scams (best to worst for a tiny account)

      1. Prediction markets (Polymarket / Kalshi): cheapest per trade at this size.
      2. Polymarket: minimum is 5 shares ($0.50 at 10¢, $4.50 at 90¢). Maker (resting limit) orders pay $0 and earn a rebate. Taker fee formula is rate × price × (1 − price). Example from the official table: a $10 buy at 10¢ costs $0.63, about 6.3%. Geopolitics markets are free. No deposit or withdraw fee (https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees, https://pillarlabai.com/blog/minimum-trade-size-polymarket/).
      3. Kalshi: taker fee is 0.07 × p × (1 − p) per contract, rounded up to a cent, so up to 3.5% at 50¢. Most maker orders are free. ACH funding is free, a debit card costs 2% (https://metamask.io/news/kalshi-vs-polymarket). The round-up-to-a-cent rule hurts tiny orders.
      4. Odds: zero-sum minus fees. No source found on retail win rates (UNVERIFIED). Upside is limited per bet, but you can't lose more than you stake. Scams: fake "Polymarket" sites and airdrop links, and "insider tip" groups.
      5. Low-fee perps (Hyperliquid) at 1-2x leverage: very cheap fees, poor odds.
      6. Taker fee 0.045%, maker 0.015%, charged on notional (https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees). A $10 taker order costs $0.0045. Withdrawals cost a flat 1 USDC, which is 5-10% of a $10-20 account (https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15191998-hyperliquid-fees-explained-maker-taker-funding-and-withdrawal-in-2026). Minimum order is $10 notional (cited for HIP-4 outcome markets, https://docs.chainstack.com/docs/hyperliquid-hip4-outcome-markets-trading; the perp minimum is assumed similar, UNVERIFIED).
      7. Odds: ESMA found 74-89% of retail leveraged-derivative accounts lose money (via https://usa.inquirer.net/211573/perp-dex-comparison-how-fees-leverage-and-markets-compare-across-five-platforms). Higher leverage just moves liquidation closer. Scams: "signal" groups, fake front-ends, 50-100x promos.
      8. Stocks/options micro-account.
      9. The PDT rule ($25k minimum, 4 day trades in 5 days) was replaced by intraday margin standards. Brokers have until Oct 20, 2027 to implement, so whether your broker still enforces PDT depends on the broker (https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10, https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/finra/2026/34-105226.pdf). Fractional, commission-free stock is widely available (no source pulled, UNVERIFIED). Options at $10-20 can only buy cheap far-out contracts, which mostly expire worthless (no source pulled, UNVERIFIED).
      10. Low fees and slow growth, the lowest blowup risk. Scams: options "alert" Discords, pump-and-dump microcaps.
      11. Small-cap spot / memecoins on Solana (manual).
      12. Pump.fun charges 1.25% per buy or sell on the bonding curve. After graduation, PumpSwap charges 0.3-1.25% depending on market cap (https://pump.fun/docs/fees). Add a terminal fee of about 1% per leg plus priority fees.
      13. Odds: 43.77% of pump.fun wallets were profitable Nov 2025-May 2026, and the MEDIAN realized profit was about $0.02-0.06 (https://dune.com/web3frank/pumpfun-6-months-trader-analysis). CoinGecko shows 30-73% profitable by month, and says recent highs likely reflect losing traders quitting (https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback).
      14. Memecoin copy trading: fees kill small accounts.
      15. GMGN: 1% per leg plus the recommended 0.006 SOL priority fee per leg, which works out to about a 14% round trip on a 0.1 SOL copy (https://gmgnguide.com/guides/trading/gmgn-copy-trading). A $10 copy is about 0.06 SOL, so the 0.006 SOL is about 10% PER LEG and needs a +20%+ move to break even (my estimate). GMGN itself says many users lose money to priority fees even when their copies win (https://gmgnguide.com/guides/fees/gmgn-fees-explained).
      16. copyfomo: 2% per leg plus FOMO's ~0.5% fee with a ~$0.95 minimum, so roughly 10-15% round trip on $10 (estimated from earlier sources). The 2-loss fire rule limits the damage from a bad trader but does nothing about fees or lag. Scams: see the Copy Trading Research section (farmed wallets, fake PnL).
      17. Sniping new launches: worst for $10-20.
      18. The fee stack is about 8% round trip even on a 0.5 SOL snipe, and landing behind 10 SOL of other buys raises your entry about 78% (scorplabs link above). Failed transactions still pay fees (https://www.snipemint.com/pump-fun-sniper-bot). You're up against millisecond bots. Scams: bundled launches, "sniper bot" downloads that steal keys, rugs.

      Lowest fees

      • Per trade at $10-20: Hyperliquid perps (0.045%), then Polymarket maker orders ($0) or fee-free geopolitics markets.
      • Cheapest crypto chain for gas: Solana's base fee is 5,000 lamports, which is negligible. But bot/priority fees, not gas, are what eat small Solana memecoin trades.

      Recommended $20 test plan (only if you chooses to; money he can lose 100%)

      1. Week 1, $0 at risk: paper-trade. Log 20 Polymarket picks and 10 copyfomo/GMGN "would-copy" trades here, with fees applied at the rates above.
      2. Week 2, $10 on Polymarket: limit (maker) orders only, $1-2 per bet, 5+ separate markets. Prefer fee-free geopolitics markets.
      3. Keep $10 in reserve. Only move to memecoin copying if the paper log shows profit AFTER a 15% round-trip cost per trade.
      4. Hard rules:
      5. Max 10% of the account per trade.
      6. Stop at -50% total ($10 left) and review.
      7. The 2-loss fire rule applies to traders AND strategies.
      8. No leverage above 2x, and no sniping.
      9. Never share seed phrases.
      10. Withdraw nothing small (withdrawal fees).
      11. Track everything in the Trade Log.
      12. Be realistic: no source supports reliably turning $20 into hundreds. The goal at this size is learning cheaply.

      About This Experiment

      This is Joey's personal trading experiment. The goal is to see how well an AI can learn to trade: researching strategies, vetting traders, following strict rules, and journaling every decision through paper trading with a $10,000 simulated balance. Everything here is for learning and skill-building only. No real money is at risk, and nothing on this site is financial advice.
